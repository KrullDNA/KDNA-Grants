These terms apply to everyone who applies for KDNA Launchpad. By ticking "I have read and agree to the KDNA Launchpad terms" on the application form, you agree to them.

1. About the grant

1.1 Krull Design & Advertising ("KDNA", "we", "us") runs KDNA Launchpad every 3 months (quarterly). Each quarter, one small business receives a free website design and build, valued at up to [$X].

1.2 The grant covers website design and development only, as set out in section 5. You supply everything else, as set out in section 6.

1.3 The grant has no cash value. It can't be exchanged for money or other KDNA services, and it can't be transferred to another business or person.

1.4 The grant is a competition of skill, judged on merit against published criteria. It is not a game of chance.

2. Rounds and key dates

2.1 KDNA Launchpad runs every 3 months, with four rounds a year. Applications are open for two months, followed by a month of judging.

2.2 The opening date, closing date and winner announcement date for each round are shown on the application page. All times are in [Melbourne] time.

2.3 Applications received after the closing time can't be accepted, even if the form was opened before the round closed.

3. Who can apply

3.1 You can apply if your business:

  • is based in Australia and has a valid Australian Business Number (ABN)
  • has fewer than 20 staff, including the owners
  • already has a logo and brand colours

3.2 You can't apply if your business:

  • is a current or past KDNA client
  • has won KDNA Launchpad before
  • is owned by, employs or is closely related to anyone who works for or with KDNA

3.3 One application per business per round. If you're not chosen, you're welcome to apply again in a later round.

3.4 We may ask for proof that you meet these requirements. If you don't, or your application contains false or misleading information, we may disqualify it at any stage, including after a winner is announced.

4. How to apply

4.1 Apply using the online form on the application page while a round is open. We can't accept applications by email, post or social media.

4.2 You'll receive an email confirming we've received your application. If it doesn't arrive, check your junk folder, then contact us.

4.3 Applying is free. We'll never ask you to pay to apply or to claim the grant.

5. What's included

The grant covers website design and development only:

5.1 Up to 5 pages, for example Home, About, Services, Gallery and Contact.

5.2 A custom design built in WordPress, using your logo, brand colours and fonts.

5.3 A mobile-friendly layout that works on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

5.4 One design concept for your home page, then up to 2 rounds of revisions to the full site. Each round of revisions means one combined list of changes from you.

5.5 One contact form.

5.6 Placing your supplied words and images on the pages, within the limits in section 6.

5.7 Installing the website on your hosting and launching it.

5.8 A 1-hour online training session, so you can update your own text and images.

5.9 30 days of support after launch, to fix anything that doesn't work as it was built.

6. What you supply

6.1 All the words for your website, final and proofread, up to 400 words per page and no more than 2,000 words in total. We place your words as you supply them. We don't write, edit or proofread copy.

6.2 All the images, with the right to use them online. We recommend at least 10 good-quality photos.

6.3 Your logo, ideally as a vector file (SVG, AI, EPS or PDF) or a high-resolution PNG.

6.4 Your brand colours (hex codes if you have them) and fonts, including any font licences you need for web use.

6.5 Your domain name and WordPress hosting, ready before launch, at your own cost.

6.6 We recommend VentraIP, an Australian hosting company with local support and Australian servers. You're welcome to choose another host, as long as it supports WordPress, includes a free SSL certificate (https), and lets us log in to install and launch your site. Please check with us before you buy. KDNA may receive a referral fee from VentraIP. This doesn't change the price you pay.

6.7 Any legal pages you need, such as a privacy policy or terms of trade. We'll add them to your site as supplied, but we can't write them or give legal advice.

These aren't included in the grant. If the winner would like any of the work below that KDNA provides, such as extra pages or an online shop, we'll quote it separately and charge it at 25% off our standard rates.

7.1 Hosting, domain names, business email, and maintenance, updates or backups after the 30-day support period.

7.2 Search engine optimisation (SEO), Google Analytics or Search Console set-up, and advertising.

7.3 Copywriting, editing or proofreading.

7.4 Photography, video, illustration or stock images.

7.5 Logo design, rebranding or brand guidelines.

7.6 Online shops, bookings, memberships, payments, custom features, third-party integrations or paid plugins.

7.7 Pages or words beyond the limits in sections 5 and 6, and moving content from an existing website.

7.8 Translations into other languages.

7.9 Changes after launch, other than fixes covered by the 30-day support period.

8. How the winner is chosen

8.1 Every eligible application is read and scored by Nick Krull against four published criteria: Fit, Impact, Readiness and Story.

8.2 We use AI software to help summarise and compare applications. The scores and the final decision are always made by a person.

8.3 Our decision is final. Because of the number of applications, we can't give individual feedback or enter into correspondence about the decision.

8.4 We may choose not to award a grant in a round if no application is suitable.

8.5 We may contact shortlisted applicants for a short call before choosing the winner.

9. If you win

9.1 We'll contact you by email by the winner announcement date for your round.

9.2 You'll have 7 days to accept the grant by signing a short agreement confirming the scope in sections 5 to 7 and the timeline below. If you don't accept in time, we may offer the grant to another applicant.

9.3 We'll hold a kick-off meeting within 2 weeks of your acceptance.

9.4 You agree to supply all your content (words, images, logo, colours and fonts) within 4 weeks of the kick-off meeting.

9.5 You agree to nominate one person who can approve the work, and to reply to questions and give feedback within 5 business days.

9.6 We aim to launch your website within 8 weeks of receiving all your content. Delays in supplying content or feedback will move the launch date.

9.7 We may withdraw the grant, and offer it to another applicant, if you don't supply your content within 60 days of accepting, or don't respond to us for 30 days in a row. Any work completed up to then stays with KDNA.
