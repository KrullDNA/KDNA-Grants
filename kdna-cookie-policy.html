Cookie policy

1. About this policy

  1. 1.1This policy explains how krulldna.com, run by Krull Design & Advertising ("we", "us"), uses cookies and similar technologies. It covers the whole website, including Launchpad and the Brand Health Score.
  2. 1.2Please read it together with our privacy policy, which explains how we collect and use personal information.

2. What cookies are

  1. 2.1Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They help the website work properly and remember things about your visit, such as your choices.
  2. 2.2Websites can also save information in your browser in other ways, such as "local storage". It does a similar job to a cookie, so in this policy we call all of these "cookies".

3. Cookies we use on our website

  1. 3.1Essential cookies. These keep the website working and secure, for example:
    • keeping our forms secure and making sure submissions are genuine
    • keeping you signed in, if you manage the website

    They can't be switched off, because the website won't work properly without them.

  2. 3.2Preference cookies. We use a cookie to remember whether you've seen our sign-up pop-up, so we don't show it to you again. It doesn't collect any information about you.
  3. 3.3Statistics cookies. [We use Google Analytics to understand how people use our website, such as which pages are visited, how long people stay and how they found us.] This information is collected on an aggregated basis and doesn't identify you. We use it to improve our website.

4. Launchpad

  1. 4.1The Launchpad application form doesn't use cookies to track you. It only uses the essential security checks described in 3.1.
  2. 4.2When you enter your ABN, we check it with the Australian Business Register from our own server. Your browser doesn't connect to the Register directly, and no cookies are set by it.

5. Brand Health Score

  1. 5.1While you take the Brand Health Score, your progress is saved in your own browser using local storage, so you can come back and carry on where you left off.
  2. 5.2This information stays on your device. It isn't sent to us until you submit your answers, and it's cleared when you finish or start again. You can also clear it at any time by clearing your browser's data for our website.

6. Other websites' cookies

  1. 6.1Some parts of our website may come from other services, such as [embedded videos, social media posts, maps or fonts]. These services may set their own cookies, which are covered by their own privacy and cookie policies, not this one.
  2. 6.2We don't use advertising or tracking cookies that follow you across other websites, and we never sell your information.

7. Managing cookies

  1. 7.1You can choose to accept, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Each browser explains how in its help pages:
    • Chrome: Settings, then Privacy and security
    • Safari: Settings (or Preferences), then Privacy
    • Firefox: Settings, then Privacy & Security
    • Edge: Settings, then Cookies and site permissions
  2. 7.2If you block essential cookies, some parts of our website, such as our forms, may not work properly.
  3. 7.3Blocking cookies on one device or browser doesn't block them on others, so you may need to change the settings on each one you use.

8. Changes to this policy

  1. 8.1We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we start using a new service on our website. We'll post the updated version on this page.
