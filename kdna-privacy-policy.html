Privacy policy

1. About this policy

  1. 1.1This page provides you with important legal and privacy information. For your own peace of mind, we encourage you to read it.
  2. 1.2The domain name krulldna.com is owned and maintained by Krull Design & Advertising ("we", "us"), and users of the site are covered by this Privacy Policy. It also covers Launchpad and the Brand Health Score.
  3. 1.3You are welcome to visit and explore our website without giving us any information about yourself. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. What information do we collect?

  1. 2.1There are four ways we may collect information from you:
    • when you contact us or we contact you
    • when you apply for Launchpad
    • when you use the Brand Health Score
    • when you browse our website

3. Information collected when you contact us

  1. 3.1When you contact us by email, phone or a form on our website, we collect the details you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, business name and the details of your enquiry.

4. Information collected for Launchpad

  1. 4.1When you apply for Launchpad, our free quarterly website programme, we collect:
    • your name, email address and phone number
    • your Australian Business Number (ABN), business name and trading name
    • your answers about your business, your goals and the website you'd like
    • your agreement to the Launchpad terms and conditions
  2. 4.2When you enter your ABN, we check it against the public Australian Business Register (ABN Lookup) to confirm it is active and to fill in your registered business name.
  3. 4.3If you're not chosen and you're eligible, we also keep a record of the discount offer we send you, and whether it was used, so each business receives one offer only.

5. Information collected for the Brand Health Score

  1. 5.1When you use the Brand Health Score, we collect your answers to the questions about your brand and your business (such as its stage, team size, plans and what you'd like help with), and your first name, email address and business name. You can also choose to give us your website address and to tell us, in your own words, what you feel your brand needs.
  2. 5.2We use this information to work out your score, show and email you your results, and, if you ask us to, follow up with suggestions on how to improve your brand. We also use your answers to judge how soon we might be able to help you, so we know who to follow up with first. This rating is only used by us and is never shown publicly.
  3. 5.3If you give us your website address, we visit your homepage once to check a few basic signs, such as whether it is secure and set up for mobile phones.

6. Information collected as you browse our website

  1. 6.1Our web server collects some anonymous information automatically when you visit our site. This may include the pages you visited, the content you downloaded, the type of web browser you were using and the level of encryption your browser supports.
  2. 6.2As you browse, our server collects information about your visit, but not about you personally. We monitor statistics such as which pages people visit, which parts of the site are visited most often and the search terms visitors use.
  3. 6.3This information is collected on an aggregated basis. No personal information is associated with these statistics. We use it to work out what information is most useful to you, so we can improve our website and make it easier to use.

7. What are cookies and how do we use them?

  1. 7.1Our website uses a cookie to remember whether you have seen our sign-up pop-up, so we don't show it to you again. We don't collect this information.
  2. 7.2While you take the Brand Health Score, your progress is saved in your own browser, so you can come back and carry on where you left off. This stays on your device, and it is cleared when you finish or start again.
  3. 7.3Cookies can also tell us which pages are visited, by how many people, the average time spent, and other useful statistics about the use of our site. We use these statistics to understand how people use our site, make it easier to use, monitor its performance and improve it.
  4. 7.4We never combine information gathered by a cookie with information that identifies you. Most websites use cookies, and most browsers are set up to accept them. If you wish, you can set your browser to accept all cookies, reject all cookies, or tell you when a cookie is sent.

8. How do we use the information we collect?

  1. 8.1We use the information we collect in a limited number of ways. We may use it to:
    • reply to your enquiry and provide our services
    • assess Launchpad applications, tell applicants the result, and send eligible applicants the discount offer described in the Launchpad terms
    • work out your Brand Health Score and send you your results
    • add you to our email list, if you ask us to, so we can send you brand tips and news
    • improve our website and services and make them easier to use
    • manage and develop our business
    • meet legal and regulatory requirements
  2. 8.2Anonymous information is used for research and analysis. We use statistics gathered at an aggregated level to understand how our site is used and to improve its flow, content, navigation and ease of use.
  3. 8.3We won't add you to a marketing mailing list unless you agree to it, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

9. Use of AI services

  1. 9.1To help us summarise and compare Launchpad applications, your answers about your business may be processed by Claude, an AI service provided by Anthropic. Your name, email address, phone number and ABN are not shared with it. The AI only helps us read and compare applications. All scores and decisions are made by a person.
  2. 9.2If you use the Brand Health Score, your answers, the results of the homepage check and anything you write in your own words may be processed by Claude to prepare your personal assessment and recommendations. Your name, email address, business name and website address are not shared with it. Your score itself is worked out by fixed rules, not by AI.

10. Disclosure of information

  1. 10.1We will not sell your personal information, and we will not disclose it except where it is necessary or permitted by law. We may disclose your personal information to:
    • you, or an agent acting on your behalf
    • service providers who help us run our website and business, such as our website host, our email marketing provider (Campaign Monitor) and the AI service described in section 9, who may only use it to provide their services to us
    • a collection agency or a party used to assess creditworthiness
    • law enforcement agencies
    • the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or another regulator
  2. 10.2If you win Launchpad, we may publish your business name, logo and website as described in the Launchpad terms and conditions.

11. Information sent overseas

  1. 11.1Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia. In particular, the AI service described in section 9 is provided by Anthropic and may process information in the United States. We only use providers we believe protect information to a standard similar to the Australian Privacy Principles.
  2. 11.2If you join our email list, your details are stored by our email marketing provider, Campaign Monitor, which may store information outside Australia.

12. How long we keep your information

  1. 12.1We keep personal information only for as long as we need it. Launchpad application records are kept for 24 months after each round is decided, then deleted. Records of discount offers that were used are kept as business records. Brand Health Score results, including unfinished attempts, are kept for 12 months and then deleted automatically. If you joined our email list, you stay on it until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.

13. Keeping your information safe

  1. 13.1We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. Our website uses a secure (https) connection, and access to applications and results is limited to Krull Design & Advertising.

14. Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

  1. 14.1You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, at any time. Email us at hello@krulldna.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If we can't do what you've asked, for example because the law requires us to keep a record, we'll tell you why.
  2. 14.2If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

15. Links to other websites

  1. 15.1Our website contains links to other sites that are not covered by this Privacy Policy, such as those of our clients, business partners and suppliers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of other sites. This policy applies only to information collected by this website or through our direct contact with you.
  2. 15.2When you click one of these links, you move to another website. Please read the privacy statement and terms of use of each site you visit, so you're comfortable with how it might use your personal information. We provide these links as a convenience only, and including a link doesn't mean we endorse, have checked or approve that website or its products, services or publications.

16. Updating this policy

  1. 16.1This policy is designed to adapt to changing technology and to people's changing expectations about privacy.
  2. 16.2We may update this policy from time to time, so please review it now and then. If we change it, we'll post the changes on this page promptly.
