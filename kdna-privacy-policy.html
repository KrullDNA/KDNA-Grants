1. Privacy policy

This page provides you with important legal and privacy information. For your own peace of mind, we encourage you to read it.

The domain name krulldna.com is owned and maintained by Krull Design & Advertising ("we", "us"), and users of the site are covered by this Privacy Policy. It also covers KDNA Launchpad and the KDNA Brand Health Score.

You are welcome to visit and explore our website without giving us any information about yourself. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. What information do we collect?

There are four ways we may collect information from you:

  • when you contact us or we contact you
  • when you apply for KDNA Launchpad
  • when you use the KDNA Brand Health Score
  • when you browse our website

2.1 Information collected when you contact us

When you contact us by email, phone or a form on our website, we collect the details you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, business name and the details of your enquiry.

2.2 Information collected for KDNA Launchpad

When you apply for KDNA Launchpad, our free quarterly website programme, we collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number
  • your Australian Business Number (ABN), business name and trading name
  • your answers about your business, your goals and the website you'd like
  • your agreement to the KDNA Launchpad terms and conditions

When you enter your ABN, we check it against the public Australian Business Register (ABN Lookup) to confirm it is active and to fill in your registered business name.

If you're not chosen and you're eligible, we also keep a record of the discount offer we send you, and whether it was used, so each business receives one offer only.

2.3 Information collected for the KDNA Brand Health Score

When you use the KDNA Brand Health Score, we collect [your name, email address and business name] and your answers to the questions about your brand. We use them to work out your score, show or send you your results, and, if you ask us to, follow up with suggestions on how to improve your brand.

2.4 Information collected as you browse our website

Our web server collects some anonymous information automatically when you visit our site. This may include the pages you visited, the content you downloaded, the type of web browser you were using and the level of encryption your browser supports.

As you browse, our server collects information about your visit, but not about you personally. We monitor statistics such as which pages people visit, which parts of the site are visited most often and the search terms visitors use.

This information is collected on an aggregated basis. No personal information is associated with these statistics. We use it to work out what information is most useful to you, so we can improve our website and make it easier to use.

3. What are cookies and how do we use them?

Our website uses a cookie to remember whether you have seen our sign-up pop-up, so we don't show it to you again. We don't collect this information.

Cookies can also tell us which pages are visited, by how many people, the average time spent, and other useful statistics about the use of our site. We use these statistics to understand how people use our site, make it easier to use, monitor its performance and improve it.

We never combine information gathered by a cookie with information that identifies you. Most websites use cookies, and most browsers are set up to accept them. If you wish, you can set your browser to accept all cookies, reject all cookies, or tell you when a cookie is sent.

4. How do we use the information we collect?

We use the information we collect in a limited number of ways. We may use it to:

  • reply to your enquiry and provide our services
  • assess KDNA Launchpad applications, tell applicants the result, and send eligible applicants the discount offer described in the KDNA Launchpad terms
  • work out your Brand Health Score and send you your results
  • improve our website and services and make them easier to use
  • manage and develop our business
  • meet legal and regulatory requirements

Anonymous information is used for research and analysis. We use statistics gathered at an aggregated level to understand how our site is used and to improve its flow, content, navigation and ease of use.

We won't add you to a marketing mailing list unless you agree to it, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Your name, email address, phone number and ABN are not shared with it. The AI only helps us read and compare applications. All scores and decisions are made by a person.
